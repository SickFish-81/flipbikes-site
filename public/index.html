One Trailer - Multiple Bikes

Experience the freedom of optimizing your trailer space without sacrificing stability. Choose FLIP Bike Chocks and revolutionize how you transport your motorcycles. With FLIP, fitting multiple bikes on a flat trailer has never been simpler!

One Trailer - Multiple Uses

Transform your trailer into a versatile, multi-use platform effortlessly, thanks to FLIP Bike Chocks' recessed design, allowing you to adapt and utilize your trailer space for various purposes when your motorcycles aren't on board.

Original & Unmatched

FLIP units are the pioneers in recessed trailer bike chocks and wheel holders. Nothing beats the original, and these units are the real deal when it comes to holding your prized motorcycles during transport.

Built to Last

Crafted in the heart of New Zealand from heavy-duty aluminum, these units are as tough as your passion for riding. Say goodbye to rust worries; these chocks are engineered to outlast your trailer, offering a lifetime of reliability.

Keep your trailer flat!

No more fussing with attachments. Flip Bike Chocks fold flat into your trailer floor when not in use and pivot open easily when needed. This means you can switch between flatbed and motorcycle transport without the hassle of bolting and unbolting.

Versatile & reliable

From dirt bikes to massive Harley Davidson roadies, FLIP units accommodate them all. Experience peace of mind knowing your front wheel won't budge during transit.

Kiwi Made

Made in the heart of the Kiwi spirit, FLIP Bike Chocks ensures a product that not only meets but exceeds industry standards.
