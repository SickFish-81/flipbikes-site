About Flip Bike Chocks

At FLIP Bike Chocks, we're not just in the business of securing motorcycles; we're driven by a lifelong passion for bikes and a commitment to safety on the road. Our story begins with Craig, the founder and creator of FLIP Bike Chocks, whose journey into the world of motorcycles started at the tender age of seven.

The Genesis

Craig's deep love for motorcycles became more than just a hobby—it became a way of life. However, it was a defining moment during a fateful trip that sparked the inception of FLIP Bike Chocks. One of Craig's prized bikes tumbled off his trailer during transit. That experience ignited a fire within him; he knew there had to be a better way to secure bikes for transport, the key to the Flip Bike Chock was Craig still needed to be able to use his trailer for other uses.  

The Innovation

Driven by his passion and a desire to solve a common problem faced by riders transporting bikes, Craig set out on a mission. The result? FLIP Bike Chocks—the original, innovative solution for recessed trailer bike chocks and wheel holders. Crafted from heavy-duty aluminum right here in New Zealand, these chocks are engineered to withstand the demands of the road while ensuring the safety and security of your beloved bikes.

Our Mission

Our mission is clear—to revolutionize how motorcycles are transported. We strive to provide riders with a reliable, space-saving solution that not only keeps their bikes secure but also simplifies the entire process. Whether you're a seasoned rider, an adventurer, or a passionate bike enthusiast, FLIP Bike Chocks is here to enhance your riding experience.
